Packing List

Packing for a cycle tour in India takes some planning, we need to be light yet carry all essential clothing and bike tools and fit all into two 20ltr panniers.

6 Jul 2026

I got all this lot into a pair of Ortlieb panniers, total 40lt. I would really recommend Ortlieb, top class panniers. I had one problem with the fixing bracket on the Indian rough roads, see the last chapter 'In Conclusion' - 'Bike Problems'.

Documents

  • Money Belt
  • Credit Card (copy number)
  • ATM Debit Card (copy number)
  • Passport (scan) - Documents scanned to upload to Mobile Me
  • Visa/Travel Permits (scan)
  • Document Copies (copies of all important documents)
  • Vaccination Records (scan)- *Rabies, Tetanus/Diphtheria/Polio, Hepatitis A + B, Typhoid, Japanese Encephalitis**
  • Motorcycle Licence (scan)
  • Airline Tickets (Scan)
  • Travel Insurance Policy (Scan)
  • Hostel Membership
  • Passport Photos
  • Glasses Prescription (Scan)
  • Small thin wallet
INFO

Where documents/papers were scanned - I scanned these to an online account ‘Mobile Me’

INFO

Vaccination notes: Most of the vaccinations are quite cheap or free. Japanese Encephalitis is very expensive, £90.00 plus and may only be needed if visiting North India - check with the NHS website or your GP surgery. I never took or used any Malaria Tablets, they are very expensive and may only be needed if visiting North India, again, check

If I went again:

  • Small electric heater - to make coffee in the morning as coffee was hard to get early in the morning especially on the east coast
  • Cup and Spoon
  • Inflatable Pillow - most pillows in India are either hard or grubby
  • A lonely planet guide book (guides you to the tourist areas of the bigger towns. I really missed a decent guide book)
